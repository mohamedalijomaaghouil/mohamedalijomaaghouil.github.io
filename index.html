CiTIUS · ROBOTA-SUDOE · 2026

The force sensor that read 100× too low

Integrating a 6-axis OnRobot HEX-E v2 force/torque sensor over EtherCAT and publishing it in ROS 2.

×100measurement error traced to byte decoding
2.77 → 0.69 Nmean error after calibration

The story

  • The sensor's readings were about 100 times too small. Instead of patching the numbers, I went down to the raw EtherCAT bytes and found the decoding error.
  • Fixed the driver, then built a 3×3 calibration.
  • Validated it on an independent dataset: mean error dropped from 2.77 N to 0.69 N.

SIGMA Clermont · EU project REMAIN-SUDOE · 2025–26

Robotic defect inspection with a UR3e

Semester 7–8 team project (3 students), ranked 2nd of 93. A UR3e with an Intel RealSense camera photographs a shoe from several angles to detect defects.

My part: where is the part?

  • 3D localisation of the part from aligned colour and depth images: separate the floor with a depth threshold, morphological filtering, contours with OpenCV.
  • Compute the part's position and orientation and project it to 3D, in a ROS 2 node (Python).
  • A live annotated control image (bounding box, distance, orientation) to follow the inspection.

My teammates handled motion planning, simulation and the deep-learning defect detection.

ENISo · 2nd-year project · Jan – May 2025

FOLLOW-ME: a person-following robot

Team of 3. A mobile robot that detects a person, starts following on a raised hand, and keeps a safe distance.

2 m ± 0.1following distance
~20 fpsprocessing rate

How it works

  • MediaPipe finds the shoulders; their midpoint gives the torso position. An Intel RealSense D435i gives its distance.
  • A hand gesture starts and stops the tracking; the robot stops safely if the person leaves the field of view.
  • Linear and angular speeds (Vx, Wz) are sent to the base over Modbus TCP.
